## How to verify your piece

1. Find it by tracking number (GODS-001 to GODS-014) on the vault site.
2. Scan its QR code — it reads the title, the edition (1 of 1), the full
   SHA-256 hash, and this vault.
3. Hash the art file you received (`sha256sum <file>`).
4. Compare. Match means authentic, one of one.

**A color change does not duplicate a piece.** A piece is its code plus
its number, not its pixels. No second certificate is ever issued for the
same code. The full hashes live in [`CERTIFICATES.md`](CERTIFICATES.md)
and the machine-readable record in [`vault.json`](vault.json).

**Museum grade** is the artist's designation. Each certificate also
carries one sealed element — the detail that makes that piece special —
printed only on the purchaser's certificate. The purchaser chooses
whether to highlight and reveal it, or keep it sealed. The sealed
elements are never in this public repo.
