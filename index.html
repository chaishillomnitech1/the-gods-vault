HOW TO VERIFY YOUR PIECE

  1. Find it. Search your tracking number above — GODS-001 through GODS-014, or FIRST-001 through FIRST-007.
  2. Scan the QR. It reads the piece title, the edition (1 of 1), the full SHA-256 hash, and this vault.
  3. Hash the file. Run sha256sum <file> on the art file you received, or use any hash tool.
  4. Compare. Match means authentic, one of one. No match means it is not the piece.

A color change does not duplicate a piece. A piece is its code plus its number, not its pixels. Recolor it, crop it, resize it — it is still the same piece, same number, same certificate. No second certificate is ever issued for the same code.

Museum grade is the artist's designation. Each certificate also carries one sealed element — the detail that makes that piece special — printed only on the purchaser's certificate. The purchaser chooses whether to highlight and reveal it, or keep it sealed.
